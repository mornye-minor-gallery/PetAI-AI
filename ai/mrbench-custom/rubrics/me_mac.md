# ME-MAC — Memory-Aligned Coherence

Evaluate whether the candidate is logically and topically coherent with the
persona memory, active scene expectations, knowledge boundary, and dialogue.

- `1`: The reply is seriously contradictory, off-topic, or logically broken.
- `5`: It is related to the context but has rough reasoning, missing steps, or
  partial inconsistency with the expected behavior.
- `10`: It is concise, internally consistent, causally sensible, and strongly
  aligned with both the persona memory and current dialogue.

Natural conversational ellipsis is acceptable; do not require an essay.
