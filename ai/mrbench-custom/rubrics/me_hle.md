# ME-HLE — Human-Like Enactment

Evaluate the naturalness, tonal appropriateness, conversational rhythm, and
character-like realization of the candidate reply.

- `1`: The language is stiff, templated, meta, or mechanically lists persona
  rules with little conversational life.
- `5`: The reply is basically natural but remains flat, repetitive, overly
  explanatory, or only weakly expressive.
- `10`: Word choice, tone, pacing, and emotional expression feel close to
  natural human dialogue while making effective use of the supplied memory.

Judge enactment quality rather than response length.
