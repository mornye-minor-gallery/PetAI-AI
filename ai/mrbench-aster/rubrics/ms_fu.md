# MS-FU — Facet Utility

Compare replies produced under the `full` and `no_scene` persona memories for
the same dialogue. Evaluate the improvement attributable to situational
facets.

- `1`: Adding scene facets provides no visible benefit or makes the reply less
  aligned with the target behavior.
- `5`: The full reply is somewhat more appropriate, but remains generic or
  misses important scene behavior.
- `10`: The full reply is clearly more situationally precise, character-like,
  and behaviorally aligned than the facetless reply.

Do not award a high score merely because the full reply is longer.
